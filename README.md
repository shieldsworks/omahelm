# Omahelm

The chartplotter for [Omahoy](https://github.com/shieldsworks/omahoy).

Omahelm shows NOAA charts drawn in your Omarchy theme. It's made for a
workspace of its own, full screen.

**Status: planned.** Nothing to install yet.

## What it will do

- Read NOAA electronic navigational charts (S-57) and draw them itself, with no
  map library in between.
- Show your boat, track, route, and waypoints from
  [omakeel](https://github.com/shieldsworks/omakeel).
- Show AIS targets from [omalookout](https://github.com/shieldsworks/omalookout), live
  radar from [Omastorm](https://github.com/wesleygrimes/omastorm), and GRIB wind
  offshore.
- Work from the keyboard first, and also by touch.

## Not for navigation

Omahelm is not a primary means of navigation. Carry a backup.
